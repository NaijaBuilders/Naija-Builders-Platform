## Why we're building this

Ask anyone who has built a house in Nigeria how they found their cement, and you'll hear something like: "I called a guy, who knew a guy." Prices change from one market to the next. A supplier who was reliable last month stops picking up the phone. Half the site's week goes on chasing quotes and waiting for a truck that was due on Monday.

NaijaBuilders puts that whole process in one place. Builders can see who sells what, compare prices, talk to suppliers directly and follow an order until it reaches the site. Suppliers get customers they'd never have met through word of mouth alone.

## Who it's for

**Builders, contractors and developers.** Whether it's a single bungalow in Enugu or a block of flats in Lekki, you can search for cement, steel, wood, tiles, aggregates, fixtures and tools, put quotes side by side, and order from suppliers whose business details we've checked.

**Suppliers and distributors.** You list your stock once and reach projects across the country. Orders, messages and enquiries all arrive in one dashboard, and your reviews build a reputation that goes wherever your business does.

**Service providers.** Not every job is about materials. Site crews, installers and project support teams can offer their services and get hired on the same platform.

## What you can do on NaijaBuilders

- Browse materials by category and compare prices from different suppliers
- Check a supplier's profile, ratings and verification before you spend a kobo
- Message suppliers directly and keep every conversation tied to the job it belongs to
- Place orders and follow delivery updates without phoning round
- Save the products you keep coming back to
- Hire construction professionals when you need hands on site

## Verified suppliers, not strangers

Trust is the hard part of buying materials. That's why suppliers go through an onboarding and verification step before they get the verified badge. It won't remove every risk. It does mean you know who you're dealing with before money changes hands.

## On the web and on your phone

Most building decisions get made on site, not at a desk. So besides the website, there's a NaijaBuilders mobile app for iOS and Android. You can browse, message suppliers and check on orders from your phone. The app is in early access right now.
